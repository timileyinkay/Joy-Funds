How funding works

Three simple steps to double your funds

Funding is automated and clear. Deposit into your Joy Funds account, wait for the government to accept it, and your balance grows on its own.

1

Deposit into your account

Send your funds to the Joy Funds PalmPay account shown in the Investment page. The name on the sending account must match the name you enter.

2

Government accepts the deposit

Your deposit is added to pending. The Government reviews it against bank records and accepts it. Once accepted, the amount is credited to your balance and your 4-day doubling timer starts.

3

Your funds double in 4 days

Within 4 days of acceptance, Joy Funds automatically doubles the deposited amount. The increase is added to your balance and appears on your dashboard.
